# List of measurable objectives, assumptions, risks and constraints.

## Suggest events

The objective is to allow users to create all types of events to bring people together.

**Risques :**

- **Content Quality :** Risk of "spam" or fictitious/scam events.

- **Physical Safety :** If anyone can create an event, how can the physical safety of participants be guaranteed?

- **Copyright :** Use of images or brand names without authorization in descriptions..

**Constraints :**

- **Moderation :** Need for a reporting and validation system (either automatic or human).

## Select an event (Filters)

The objective is to ensure discoverability and relevance of events for the user.

**Risks :**

- **Overload :** Too many filters can confuse the user; too few can frustrate them if there are too many events.

**Constraints :**

- **Technical Performance :** Database queries with multiple criteria (date, location, category, price) must be ultra-fast.

- **Real-time Updates :** A full or canceled event must disappear or update instantly.

## Complete messaging (Individual and Group)

The objective is to drive engagement and coordination.

**Risks :**

- **Harassment :** Event groups can become unsafe spaces.

- **Data Breach :** Private conversations are targets for hacking.

**Constraints :**

- **Storage :** Managing message history, images, and attachments requires a robust database.

- **GDPR :** Legal obligation to secure exchanges and allow for data deletion.

## Personal profile

L'objectif est l'identité et la confiance au sein du réseau.

**Risks :**

- **Identity Theft :** Creation of fake profiles.

**Constraints :**

- **Permission Management :** Creating privacy settings (who can see my profile, my friends, my events).

- **Verification :** Implementing (potentially) a certification system to reassure members.
