
# Cherry 🍒

![Logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LoucheVDC/Assets/main/cherry.png)


## Documentation

Our project is somewhat similar to Meetup, but with a fresh and more interactive approach. The concept is simple: Cherry gets your location and proposes events nearby that match your interests. But what sets our app apart from others is not just proximity—it’s the way you discover and engage with events.  

Unlike other event apps, Cherry focuses on spontaneity, fun, and a visually engaging experience. With features inspired by popular social apps like Tinder, users can swipe through events and quickly find the ones that excite them.  

## Features

- **Swipe functionality** – Our main difference is the addictive swipe feature, making discovering events playful and fast.  
- **Easy and fun to use** – Minimal learning curve and a visually appealing interface.  
- **Spontaneous events** – Users can quickly join or create events on the fly.  
- **Cross-platform** – Works seamlessly on iOS, Android, and web.  
- **Rich media content** – Lots of images, making events visually engaging.  
- **Group participation** – Friends can join events as a group, enhancing social experiences.  
- **Event ratings & feedback** – After events, users can rate events and participants using emojis.  
- **Notifications & reminders** – Personalized alerts ensure users never miss an event.  
- **Search & filter** – Quickly find events by category, date, or location.  
- **Social sharing** – Share events with friends and on social media.  

## User Experience & Target Audience

Cherry targets a younger, tech-savvy audience but is designed to be accessible for everyone. Short tutorials and clear instructions make it easy for new users to navigate the app and create or join events. The combination of a fun interface, swipe-based discovery, and social features makes the experience enjoyable for all users.  

## Conclusion

Cherry is more than just another event app. While the core concept resembles Meetup, our app brings a unique, interactive experience with advanced features and a playful interface. Inspired by Tinder’s swipe-based design, Cherry makes discovering events exciting and engaging. Users will love the ease of joining spontaneous events, connecting with friends, and exploring new experiences. With its rich media content, group functionalities, and personalized recommendations, Cherry is poised to become a favorite app for social, outgoing, and adventurous individuals.
