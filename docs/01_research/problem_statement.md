# Problem statement

## 1. The Problem

We are more connected digitally than ever, yet more isolated physically. Many people want to go out but stay home because they have no one to join them.

The transition from online to real-life is broken. Current social networks prioritize scrolling and content consumption over real human interaction.

---

## 2. Target Audience

Isolation affects all age groups:

- Students & Newcomers: Moving to a new city and starting over.
- Workers & Telecommuters: Stuck in "home-work" routines with no social outlets.
- Seniors: Facing family or geographic isolation and wanting to stay active.
- Introverts: Needing a specific activity (sport, meal, hobby) to break the ice.

---

## 3. Impact

Lack of social connection leads to:

- Mental Health: Loneliness, social anxiety, and depression.
- Life Balance: A life limited to work and screens without meaningful sharing.
- Physical Health: Sedentary behavior (sitting or lying down) and lack of movement.
- Social Decline: Generations stop mingling, and local community life weakens.

---

## 4. Problem Statement

"People of all ages need a simple way to join real-life activities nearby, because current platforms do not help create the spontaneous social links needed to fight isolation."
