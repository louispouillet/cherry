    - What features would you like to see ?
    - What bothers you the most in web applications ?

- <ins>***Answer :***</ins>
  -  He uses it very often, to chat with friends and look at the reels or publications of other.
  -  He finds it quite difficult to find people to go out with because he doesn't know many people and in the university, people already have their group of friends.
  -  Sometimes, he does but it is not the main purpose why he uses it.
  -  He would because it make him go out and see new people, and discover new things. Maybe the weekend or when he can during the week.
