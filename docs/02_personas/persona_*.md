# Personas :

The personas created based on the interviews conducted to better understand the target users for our web application.

## Persona 1 – Young Social Explorer

### Profile
- Age: 18–35
- Student or young professional
- Introverted / socially reserved or not
- Very active on social media

### Goals
- Expand their social circle
- Discover new activities and events
- Go out and socialize more often

### Motivations
- Meet people with similar interests
- Reduce feelings of loneliness
- Build friendships outside work

### Behaviours
- Uses mobile apps daily
- Searches online for events and activities
- Hesitates to attend events alone
- Avoids apps with intrusive ads

### Pain Points
- Difficulty meeting new people naturally
- Too many ads in social apps
- Fear of awkward social interactions
- Overwhelming or cluttered interfaces

---

## Persona 2 – Busy Social Planner

### Profile
- Age: 36–65
- Working professional or parent
- Uses social media occasionally
- Prefers face-to-face interactions

### Goals
- Socialize during weekends or holidays
- Find well-organized activities
- Balance social life with work and family

### Motivations
- Spend quality time with others
- Meet people in a structured and safe environment
- Make efficient use of limited free time

### Behaviours
- Plans activities in advance
- Uses web applications occasionally
- Reads notifications carefully
- Avoids apps that send too many alerts

### Pain Points
- Lack of time to search for events                                                                                                         
- Too many notifications
- Concerns about data privacy
- Unreliable or poorly organized activities

---

## Persona 3 – Active Retiree

### Profile
- Age: 66+
- Retired
- Rarely uses web applications
- Has a lot of free time

### Goals
- Meet new people
- Stay socially active
- Make good use of free time

### Motivations
- Avoid loneliness
- Stay mentally and socially engaged
- Enjoy traveling and discovering new places

### Behaviours
- Uses the web only when necessary
- Prefers simple and clear interfaces
- Needs guidance when using new technologies
- Attends daytime activities more often

### Pain Points
- Difficulty using complex applications
- Small text and unclear navigation
- Fear of making mistakes online
- Limited confidence with technology

---

## Persona 4 – New City Newcomer

### Profile
- Age: 18–50
- Recently moved to a new city
- Moved for work or studies
- Has no established social network locally

### Goals
- Build a social network in a new environment
- Meet people with similar interests
- Discover local events and activities

### Motivations
- Feel integrated into the new city
- Reduce isolation after moving
- Feel safe while meeting new people

### Behaviours
- Actively searches for local events
- Uses apps to find communities
- Participates in group activities
- Checks safety and moderation features

### Pain Points
- Feeling isolated in a new place
- Difficulty trusting new people
- Lack of information about local events
- Concerns about safety and security
